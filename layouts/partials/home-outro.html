Lotto Games Draw Schedule

The 2D and 3D lotto games are drawn daily at 2 PM, 5 PM, and 9 PM. All other games are drawn three (3) times a week during the 9 PM evening draw.

  • 2D EZ2 Lotto — drawn daily at 2 PM, 5 PM, and 9 PM
  • 3D Swertres Lotto — drawn daily at 2 PM, 5 PM, and 9 PM
  • 4D Lotto — drawn every Monday, Wednesday, and Friday at 9 PM
  • 6D Lotto — drawn every Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday at 9 PM
  • 6/42 Lotto — drawn every Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday at 9 PM
  • 6/45 Mega Lotto — drawn every Monday, Wednesday, and Friday at 9 PM
  • 6/49 Super Lotto — drawn every Tuesday, Thursday, and Sunday at 9 PM
  • 6/55 Grand Lotto — drawn every Monday, Wednesday, and Saturday at 9 PM
  • 6/58 Ultra Lotto — drawn every Tuesday, Friday, and Sunday at 9 PM

Where to Claim Lotto Prizes

  • For prizes up to ₱10,000.00, you may claim them at any PCSO-authorized lotto outlet.
  • For prizes from ₱10,001.00 up to ₱300,000.00, you may claim them at the PCSO Branches in your area.
  • For prizes above ₱300,000.00 up to the jackpot prize, you may claim them only at the PCSO Head Office in Mandaluyong City.
